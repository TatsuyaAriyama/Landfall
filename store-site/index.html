Privacy Policy

Your records live on your device. Signing in is optional and powers cross-device sync and Harbor features. KeelMira never tracks you and never sells your data.

  • What we collect (only if you sign in): your email address and name, user ID, study content, Harbor profile and shared records, private chat, shared-timer posts, and safety reports. It is used only for app functionality, safety, and support.
  • Where it's stored: on your device, and — when signed in — in Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Traffic is encrypted.
  • Sharing: data is shared only when you join a harbor with an invite code, and only with that harbor's members (your current-month records). Leave the harbor and that shared copy is removed.
  • No tracking: no ad networks, no Firebase Analytics product, and no cross-app tracking.
  • Deletion: choose "Delete account" in Settings to remove your account, synced and shared data, chat, and shared-timer posts, revoke Apple tokens, and cancel an active Web Voyage Pass. Safety reports may be retained only when legally or operationally necessary. If you use KeelMira without signing in, nothing leaves your device.

Google Sign-In and Firebase may handle provider account details, device identifiers, usage data, and technical diagnostics for authentication, abuse prevention, and service quality. KeelMira does not use this information for advertising or tracking.

The full policy (English and 日本語) is available in the app repository. This policy may be updated if the app's functionality changes.
