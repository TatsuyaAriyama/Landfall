# Final image-generation prompt set

Tool: built-in image generation. Existing navigator images were supplied as character references. Every image was generated on a flat green chroma-key background and converted locally to a transparent PNG.

## Shared specification

Create one image in a cohesive wordless messaging-sticker set. Match the established navigator character identity, palette, proportions, gently faceted hand-painted rendering, and thick warm-ivory sticker outline from the supplied references.

Character invariants: a small round fictional navigator wearing a terracotta pointed hood and A-line coat; a dark featureless face opening with exactly two simple ivory eyes and no mouth; cream rope collar; a large readable circular brass medallion with an eight-point compass star; deep navy cape; brown mittens, roomy trousers and boots; and a tiny warm lantern. Keep the same character in every image and do not redesign it.

Composition: one centered complete character, a bold silhouette that remains legible at small size, and generous padding with nothing cropped.

Backdrop: perfectly flat solid `#00ff00` chroma-key background, exactly uniform with no shadow, gradient, texture, floor, reflection, or lighting variation. Do not use green in the character.

Prohibitions: no text, letters, numbers, punctuation, speech balloons, logos, watermark, cast shadow, or contact shadow.

## Per-image direction

1. **Salute / understood** — Full-body three-quarter view, one mitten raised in a crisp friendly sailor salute, firm nod, chest slightly forward, confident planted stance, lively confident eyes.
2. **Thanks** — Gentle bow, one mitten over the compass medallion, the other holding the lantern low, relaxed shoulders, softened lowered eyes. Clearly gentler than the apology pose.
3. **Greeting** — Leaning forward slightly and waving one mitten high in a broad friendly arc, the other hand steadying the lantern, welcoming posture, cape lightly lifted, joyful upward-curved eyes.
4. **Apology** — Very deep full-body bow, hood and shoulders lowered, both hands folded together in front, lantern dim and low, small remorseful eyes. Clearly deeper than the gratitude pose.
5. **Joy / success** — Compact upward hop, both arms thrown high, boots just off the ground, cape and hood bouncing, lantern swinging safely from the wrist, joyful crescent eyes; no confetti or floating symbols.
6. **Encouragement** — Strong forward stance, one mitten clenched near the chest, the other holding the lantern forward like a guiding light, cape angled back, friendly determined eyes; supportive rather than angry.
7. **Sad** — Shoulders and hood drooping forward, knees slightly inward, hands loosely gathered near the abdomen, lantern hanging low with faint light, small downward-curved eyes; quiet rather than melodramatic.
8. **Sleep** — Comfortable seated sleeping pose, knees drawn inward, cape wrapped around the body like a blanket, hood tilted to one side, peaceful closed eyes, lantern upright beside the right boot with a low glow; no sleep symbols, moon, or stars.
