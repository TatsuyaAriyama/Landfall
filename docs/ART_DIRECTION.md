# Landfall art direction

## Decorative flags

- Do not add flags, pennants, banners, or flagpoles to ships, tents, piers, buildings, or newly created props.
- Communicate identity through silhouette, material, light, wear, color blocking, and functional hardware instead.
- A flag may appear only when it is an explicit functional or narrative requirement approved for that specific feature; it is not a default decoration.

## Harbor camping equipment

- Camping props should read as practical outdoor equipment, not Japanese traditional architecture.
- Separate materials visibly: synthetic flysheet, breathable inner fabric, waterproof floor, aluminum poles, reflective cord, and metal stakes.
- Every prop needs a use in the harbor. The tent is an enterable rest place, not background decoration.

## Navigator

- The navigator is a small hooded traveler, not a mascot animal or a realistic human.
- Keep the signature silhouette: coral hooded coat, midnight swallow-tail cape, glowing sand-colored eyes, chest compass, and a hand lantern.
- The face stays in shadow. Do not add a mouth, nose, hair, or realistic skin features.
- New Blender assets must keep a ground-level origin, a named rig, and explicit hand/back sockets so tools and future movement animations do not rely on guessed coordinates.
